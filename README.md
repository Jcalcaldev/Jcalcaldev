## Hi, I'm Juan

I'm a backend engineer based in Lisbon. I like building the parts of a product that everything else depends on, and then keeping them running.

For the past four years I was part of the founding team at Skillcap, working on The Beacon, an online game with a web platform around it. I looked after pretty much everything outside the game client: the APIs, the database, the job queues and the smart contracts.

A few things I'm proud of from that time:

- A backend that got us through events peaking around 220K daily active users, with about 1.3M registered accounts, without meaningful downtime
- Smart contracts behind more than $7M USD in sales, including a sale that did about $4M in one hour without a single issue
- A purchasing system that accepted both on-chain and off-chain payments, flexible enough that the game team reused it for their in-game shops
- Rebuilding our data layer as we grew, cutting read times by about 90%, and later moving it to Hasura and PostgreSQL triggers
- Job queues that handled sales, item transfers and on-chain syncing for four years without losing data

Most of my work lives in private repos, so there isn't much to see here.

I mostly work with TypeScript, Node.js, PostgreSQL, GraphQL and Redis.
